Overview

Minecraft Explorers is a cooperative card game for 1–4 players. Everyone wins or loses together. Your goal is to fill all but one of the Chest cards before the Game Over card is revealed from the Mob deck.

📷 Image: full game layout on a table

The Board

The digital board mirrors the physical game layout:

  • Chests — the cards you are working to fill. Shown at the top.
  • Mob Deck & Mobs — the threat. One new mob is revealed each turn.
  • Landscapes — five cards face-up showing locations you can explore for items.
  • Inventory — your items and mob trophies. Shown at the bottom.

Hunger

Everything costs Hunger 🍖. You start each turn with 6 Hunger. Hunger resets at the start of every new turn and cannot be saved. Food items can give you bonus Hunger during your turn.

💡 Hunger is the main resource. Plan your turn so you can afford the actions that matter most before you run out.

Setup

When you tap Create World from the start screen, the game sets everything up automatically. Here's what happens behind the scenes so you understand what you're looking at:

Mob Deck

All mob cards are shuffled. A number of cards are removed depending on player count (more players = fuller deck). The Game Over card is then inserted somewhere in the last few cards of the deck — you never know exactly when it will appear.

Landscape Deck

All landscape cards are shuffled. Five are dealt face-up to the board. Destination cards (Woodland Mansion, Ocean Monument) go to the Destinations zone if drawn; otherwise they stay in the deck until found with a Map.

Starting Mob

One mob card is revealed face-up on the board at the start of the game before any turns are taken.

✅ Beginner mode is recommended for your first game — it uses a known set of chests and has fewer of them to fill.

Your Turn

On your turn you can take as many actions as you can afford. There is no limit on the number of actions per turn — only your remaining Hunger limits you. You can repeat the same action multiple times if you have enough Hunger.

The five actions available each turn are:

  • 🗺️ Explore a Landscape — spend Hunger, collect an item
  • 🔄 Refresh Landscapes — spend 1 Hunger to replace unwanted landscapes
  • ⚔️ Fight a Mob — spend Hunger equal to the mob's cost to defeat it
  • 📦 Fill Chests — spend 1 Hunger to place items into chest slots
  • 🌙 End Turn — reveal a new mob card and pass to the next player

💡 You can use food items, swords, pickaxes, crossbows, maps, armor, and the spyglass at any time during your turn — these don't cost a separate action.

Multiplayer Turn Order

Players take turns in the order they were entered at setup. The current player's name is shown in the top-left of the info bar. After a player ends their turn, play passes to the next player who starts fresh with 6 Hunger.

Explore Landscapes

Tap any face-up Landscape card to explore it. Each landscape shows its Hunger cost and what item it contains. After paying the cost, the card flips to reveal the item — which is automatically added to your inventory.

📷 Image: example landscape card face

Villages

Village landscapes work differently — instead of paying Hunger alone, you also trade an item from your inventory. Tap a Village card to see what the villager wants and what they'll give you in return. You can trade either a matching item or an Emerald.

📷 Image: example village card

Tool Discounts

Some landscapes have a tool symbol (pickaxe or sword). If you have a matching tool in your inventory, you can use it to reduce the Hunger cost. When you tap the landscape, dropdowns will appear for each eligible tool showing how many times you want to use it.

🔧 Tools can be used multiple times in one action — even twice for the same explore. A tool used once becomes damaged; used again it breaks and is discarded.

Mob Reveals

Some landscapes have a mob icon — exploring them forces you to reveal mob cards from the Mob deck. After the item card is revealed, tap Reveal Mobs and then tap the deck to flip each mob one at a time. If an Overrun occurs (a duplicate mob type), keep tapping until the overrun resolves.

⚠️ If you have Armor in your inventory when a mob reveal is required, you can discard the armor instead to skip the reveal entirely.

Special Destinations

The Woodland Mansion and Ocean Monument are special landscapes that start hidden in the deck. Use an Explorer Map to find and place one on the board. These have higher costs and multiple mob reveals but contain unique items needed for specific chests.

Refresh Landscapes

Tap the Refresh Landscapes button to enter selection mode. The board dims and the landscape zone comes to the front.

Tap any landscape card you want to discard — it will be highlighted. Tap it again to deselect. When ready, tap Refresh Selected to remove the chosen cards and draw replacements from the deck. All empty spaces (including previously explored spots) are also filled.

💡 This action costs 1 Hunger regardless of how many cards you replace. Use it when the current landscapes don't suit your needs or when you want to fill in empty spots left by previous explores.

If a destination card (Woodland Mansion or Ocean Monument) is drawn from the deck during a refresh, it goes directly to the Destinations zone — it doesn't fill a regular landscape slot.

Fight Mobs

Tap any revealed Mob card on the board to fight it. Each mob shows its Hunger cost. Pay that cost to defeat the mob and add it to your Trophies.

Using Swords

If you have a Sword in your inventory, a discount dropdown appears when fighting. Select how many times you want to use the sword to reduce the Hunger cost. Multiple swords can be used for the same fight.

Overrun

When a new mob is revealed that matches a mob type already on the board, Overrun occurs. Another mob must be drawn immediately. This chains until a non-duplicate mob (or the Game Over card) is drawn. Pay attention to what mob types are already on the board — having many of the same type on the board makes overrun more likely.

⚠️ The Game Over card can appear during an Overrun chain — the moment it is revealed, the game ends immediately.

Fill Chests

Tap any Chest card to open the fill menu. Chest cards show two item slots — each requiring a specific category of item (wood, iron, lighting, etc.).

📷 Image: example chest card showing empty slots

For each unfilled slot a dropdown appears showing matching items from your inventory. Select the item you want to place. You can fill one or both slots in the same action. Tap Place Items to confirm.

💡 Filling chests costs 1 Hunger total — not per item or per chest. Once you've paid, you can continue placing items in other chests at no additional cost before taking your next action.

When a Chest is Complete

When all slots in a Chest are filled, the chest closes. As a reward, you immediately get to eliminate one Mob from the board for free — tap the mob you want to defeat. If there are no mobs on the board, this reward is forfeit.

✅ Any type of food card can fill a food slot. Any quality of tool (iron, gold, diamond — even damaged) can fill a tool slot.

Special Items

Two chests require unique items found only in special destinations:

  • Wet Sponge — found only in the Ocean Monument
  • Totem of Undying — found only in the Woodland Mansion

You'll need to use an Explorer Map to access those locations.

Trophies

When you defeat a mob it becomes a Trophy in your inventory. Trophies are kept between turns.

Selling Trophies

Tap any Trophy card to see your options. If you have two trophies of the same mob type, you can discard both to gain +1 Hunger for the rest of your current turn.

💡 You can save trophies to sell on a future turn when you need the extra Hunger — you don't have to sell them immediately.

Trophies can also be saved to fill Chest slots that require mob trophies (if any such chests are in play).

Special Items

🛡️ Armor

Armor can be used in two situations:

  • When exploring a landscape that requires a mob reveal — discard armor to skip the reveal entirely.
  • When ending your turn — discard armor to skip the required mob reveal at end of turn.

When armor is available, a Use Armor button will appear alongside the mob reveal option.

🏹 Crossbow

Tap the crossbow in your inventory to use it. Select up to two mobs from the board — those mobs are defeated at no Hunger cost. The crossbow is then discarded. If there are no mobs on the board, the crossbow is still discarded but you gain no trophies.

🗺️ Explorer Map

Tap the Explorer Map to search for the Woodland Mansion or the Ocean Monument. The chosen destination is pulled from the landscape deck and placed on the board in the Destinations zone. You can then explore it like any other landscape.

⚠️ There is only one Woodland Mansion and one Ocean Monument in the deck. If one has already been found (by map or by chance), it won't appear again.

🔭 Spyglass

Tap the Spyglass to temporarily reveal the items hidden on all current landscape cards. This gives the whole team a chance to see what's available before deciding where to explore. After everyone has looked, the landscapes revert and the spyglass is discarded.

🍖 Food Items

Tap any food item in your inventory to use it and gain its Hunger bonus immediately. The bonus applies for the rest of your current turn only — unused Hunger is lost at end of turn.

🏆 Totem of Undying & 🧽 Wet Sponge

These unique items are found only in the Woodland Mansion and Ocean Monument respectively. They have no special effect other than being the only items that can fill certain specific chest slots. There is no reason to collect them unless you need them for a chest.

End of Turn

When you are done taking actions, tap End Turn. You will be asked to confirm — this is also when you can use Armor to skip the mob reveal.

Mob Reveal

At the end of every turn, one card is revealed from the Mob deck. Tap the deck card to flip it and see what appears. If it triggers Overrun, keep tapping until the chain resolves.

⚠️ If the Game Over card is revealed at any point — during end of turn OR during a landscape mob reveal — the game ends immediately. Everyone loses.

Turn Reset

After the mob reveal, the turn passes to the next player. Their Hunger resets to 6 and a brief transition screen appears. The board then re-renders showing the current state for the new active player.

Win & Lose

🏆 Winning

The players win immediately when all but one of the Chest cards on the board are fully filled. You don't need to fill every chest — just enough to leave only one unfilled.

✅ Example on Normal difficulty: 6 chests are in play. Fill any 5 of them and the game ends in victory.

💀 Losing

The Game Over card is shuffled into the bottom portion of the Mob deck before the game begins. The moment it is revealed — whether at end of turn or during a landscape mob reveal — the game ends immediately and all players lose.

⚠️ The Game Over card can appear during an Overrun chain. Every flip of the mob deck carries risk as the deck gets smaller.

Starting Over

After a win or loss, tap Play Again to return to the start screen and set up a new game.

💡 The mob deck count shown in the info bar (⚔️) tells you how many cards remain. A small number means the Game Over card is close.
